Refunds

Last updated 14 October 2026.

This app does not take payment

MediTrack does not sell goods, subscriptions, credits, or downloads. There is no cart, no price, and no payment form. Because nothing is charged here, there is nothing for this app to refund.

This is an honest notice. It is not a 7-day or 30-day money-back promise, and it must not be read as one.

Hospital bills are separate

A patient’s bill, deposit, discount, or refund is handled by Sir Ganga Ram Hospital’s billing process, not by this follow-up list. A note in MediTrack does not create a charge and does not cancel one.

For a billing question, use the hospital’s own billing desk. The Corporate Desk phones in this app are for admission follow-up: +91 9560433511 and 011-42253036 / 3037. Hospital switchboard: 011-42254000.

If payment is added later

Do not take money through this app until a real refund policy, the seller’s legal name, and the details required by the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 are published. Those rules are not switched on by this page, because this app is not an e-commerce shop today.
