Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026. This notice describes what this desk tool actually does. It is not a certificate of compliance.

MediTrack is a staff follow-up tool for the Corporate & International Desk at Sir Ganga Ram Hospital, New Delhi. It is not the hospital’s official website and it is not the hospital’s official medical record.

Who this notice is for

Desk staff who sign in, and patients whose admission follow-up is entered by that desk. Patients do not create their own accounts here.

Who is responsible

The Corporate & International Desk, Sir Ganga Ram Hospital, Sir Ganga Ram Hospital Marg, Old Rajinder Nagar, New Delhi 110060, uses this tool.

Case records are also stored in Google Firebase project meditrack-14e82. The Google account that owns that project controls the cloud copy. Patient names and phone numbers must not be written into the website’s source code. This page does not name a Data Protection Officer, because one is not published in the app. The hospital must confirm, in writing, who the data fiduciary is under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Data the app stores

  • Staff sign-in: email address and password. The password is handled by Firebase Authentication. This app does not keep its own copy of the password.
  • Patient follow-up: name, registration number, employee id, company, company email, phone number if the desk types one, treating doctor, admission date and time, ward or room, country, approval and follow-up dates, and remarks.
  • Desk reference notes the staff save, such as company entitlement text.
  • On the device: a local copy of those records, the last tab you opened, and the sign-in session, so the desk can keep working if the network drops.

Data the app does not collect

No advertising profile, no analytics product, no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, and no precise location. Do not type Aadhaar numbers, card numbers, passport scans, or a full clinical diagnosis into remarks. The form does not ask for them.

Why it is used

To track admission approvals, follow-ups, and international arrival timing for this desk, and to draft emails the staff member chooses to send. That is the only purpose.

Who else can see it

  • Every person with a MediTrack sign-in on this same Firebase workspace can see the same cases. There are no private per-patient folders inside the app.
  • Google processes the Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore data as the cloud host.
  • If a staff member drafts an email, the case details are handed to the phone or computer’s mail app. MediTrack does not run its own mail server. The staff member chooses the recipient.
  • This app does not sell personal data.

Where it is stored

On the device you are using, and in Google Firebase. This policy does not claim the cloud copy stays in India. The hospital should confirm the Firestore region before treating this tool as an approved store of patient information.

How long it is kept

A case stays until a signed-in user deletes it. There is no automatic deletion. Hospital rules may require the official medical record to be kept for longer, in the hospital’s own system. Deleting a row here does not delete the hospital record.

Security, stated plainly

Sign-in is required on the live desk. The browser keeps a local copy. These are ordinary product features, not a promise of a particular security certification. Staff must not share passwords, and must sign out on a shared phone.

Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, a person can ask the data fiduciary for access, correction, erasure, and grievance redress, subject to the Act and the rules. A patient should ask the hospital, not a public web form. This app has no public “download my data” button. Desk staff can correct or delete a case they are allowed to handle.

Children

The tool is for hospital staff. A patient may be a minor. Staff should still enter only what the desk needs for follow-up.

People outside India

The desk is in New Delhi and the tool is for staff working there. Treatment at the hospital does not, by itself, make this page a European service. If staff email a patient’s details to a person or authority outside India, that sending is a separate disclosure. The desk must have a proper reason for it.
