Cookie policy

Last updated 14 October 2026.

Is a cookie consent banner required?

No advertising or analytics cookies are used. The storage below is needed for sign-in, the case list, and the installed app. Indian law does not require a marketing-style Accept or Reject banner for that kind of storage. A short notice is shown once so staff can read this page. “Understood” only hides the notice. It does not turn on any extra tracking, because there is none to turn on.

If analytics, ads, or embedded video are added later, stop and ask for a real consent choice before those cookies run.

Third parties

The live desk loads Firebase scripts from Google (www.gstatic.com) and talks to Google Firebase for sign-in and the database. Those connections are part of running the desk. There is no Google Analytics tag, no advertising pixel, and no embedded map, video, or social feed.

How to clear it

Use the browser’s site settings for this app and clear cookies and site data, or remove the installed home-screen app. Clearing site data signs you out and removes the on-device copy. The cloud copy remains until a signed-in user deletes those cases or the Firebase project is closed.
