What MediTrack is

MediTrack is a follow-up list for the Corporate & International Desk at Sir Ganga Ram Hospital, New Delhi. It reminds staff about approvals, calls, and timing. It does not admit a patient, bill a patient, or decide clinical care.

What it is not

  • Not the official medical record. The hospital’s own system remains the record.
  • Not medical, legal, or insurance advice.
  • Not a shop. Nothing is sold in the app. See the refund notice.
  • Not the Red Cross, Red Crescent, or any other protected emblem. The red-square icon is only a desk mark.

Who may use it

Only people the desk admin has given an account. Signing in means you agree to these terms and you have read the privacy policy. You will not share the password. You will not enter a patient out of curiosity.

Patient information

Before saving a case you must confirm you are allowed to record it. Enter only what the desk needs: identity for the admission, the company or country, the doctor, dates, and a short remark. Do not paste Aadhaar, card numbers, or a full diagnosis.

Company entitlement text in the desk reference is a working note. It can be wrong or out of date. Staff must still verify the letter, the scheme, and the current hospital rate.

Emails

Drafts open in your own mail app. You are responsible for the recipient and for the text you send. MediTrack does not send that mail itself.

Accounts and availability

The admin can remove an account. The tool can be offline, wrong, or interrupted. Do not rely on it as the only reminder for a statutory or clinical deadline.
